# Data

The three real corpora that ship with the package, what each is for, the contract they load under, and
the portable form fitted arms are exported in.

1. [The corpus](data/01_the-corpus.md), 97 bench blasts, five corrected transcription defects, three
   defects in the published validation that are carried deliberately, and the two-part integrity gate.
2. [Geometry reconstruction](data/02_geometry-reconstruction.md), recovering absolute patterns from a
   dimensionless table, and the fifteen published constraints that show it is right.
3. [The portable export](data/03_portable-export.md), every fitted learned arm as plain JSON, and the
   three numeric details that make a browser port agree to the last bit.
